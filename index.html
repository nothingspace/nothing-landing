The tour

Seven parts of your day. One app to hold them.

01Feed

Everything lands in your Feed.

  • Grid
  • List
  • Timeline
  • Checklists
  • Drawings

02Voice

Talk. It types.

Hit record and read the transcript as you speak, then drop the text straight into a note. Recording carries on in a small pill while you do something else.

  • Live transcript (beta)
  • Voice to note
  • Floating recorder

03Tasks

Overdue, today, tomorrow. Already sorted.

Tasks group themselves by due date. Give one a priority, a repeat and a reminder, and Nothing nudges you on time.

FAQ

Straight answers.

Is Nothing free?

Nothing is free to download on the App Store and Google Play, and the free app shows ads. Nothing Pro is an optional monthly or yearly subscription that unlocks the premium features.

How does the AI work, and what does it cost?

The assistant runs on Google Gemini. To use it, sign in and subscribe to Pro, or add your own Gemini API key.

AI is entirely optional. Everything else in the app works without it.

Where is my data kept?

On your phone. Notes, tasks, habits and spending live in a database on your device. Turn on Backup Sync and a copy goes to your own Google Drive.

Data goes to Google’s Gemini API only when you start an AI action yourself, never in the background. The privacy policy has the details.

Do I need an account?

No. Notes, tasks, habits, money and tools work without signing in. You sign in to use the AI assistant, and connect a Google account if you want backups in Google Drive.

Is there a web or desktop version?

Not today. Nothing is a mobile app for iPhone, iPad and Android.

Which languages does it speak?

Fifteen: English, Arabic, Chinese (Simplified and Traditional), French, German, Hindi, Italian, Japanese, Korean, Portuguese (Brazil), Russian, Spanish, Thai and Vietnamese. Arabic reads right to left, and any language can switch to right-to-left.
